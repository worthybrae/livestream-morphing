Select: point at a thing and it lights up; click to keep its outline. If it lit the wrong amount, the up and down arrows step to the bigger or smaller thing there. Zoom in on small or thin things: the model looks harder at what fills the screen.

Draw: sketch roughly along a line and the stroke jumps onto the nearest contour; hold Option to draw freely. For a thing the night hides, switch to the day view. Pinch or Cmd+scroll to zoom. Everything saves as you go.
